# Privacy Policy
**QuickBooks Invoice Utility**

**Owner:** Next View Software  
**Effective Date:** November 13, 2026

## 1. Purpose

The QuickBooks Invoice Utility is an internal tool used by authorized Next View Software personnel to access and update invoice information in the company's QuickBooks Online account.

This policy describes how information is accessed and used through the utility.

## 2. Information Accessed

With authorization from the company's QuickBooks Online account, the utility may access invoice information necessary to perform its intended functions.

This may include:

- QuickBooks invoice IDs and invoice numbers.
- Invoice dates and other invoice details needed to identify records.
- Existing custom field values.
- Customer references associated with invoices.

The utility is specifically intended to update the **Service Location** custom field on invoices.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through QuickBooks is used solely for authorized internal business operations, including identifying invoices and updating their Service Location values.

The information is not used for advertising, marketing, or sale to third parties.

## 4. Data Sharing

The utility communicates with Intuit's QuickBooks Online service through its official APIs.

QuickBooks data is transmitted to Intuit as necessary to retrieve and update invoice records. Intuit's handling of that information is governed by Intuit's applicable privacy policies and terms.

The utility is not intended to share invoice information with other external parties.

## 5. Authentication and Security

The utility uses OAuth authentication provided by Intuit.

Access tokens and refresh tokens grant access to the company's QuickBooks data and must be protected against unauthorized access.

Next View Software restricts use of the utility to authorized personnel and expects credentials to be handled securely.

## 6. Data Retention

The utility is intended to process QuickBooks information for the purpose of performing authorized invoice updates. Any additional retention of information, such as operational logs, is subject to Next View Software's applicable internal practices.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

Next View Software may update this policy as the utility or its data-handling practices change.
